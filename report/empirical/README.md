# report/empirical/

Drafts for *"The Silence of the Satisfied"* — the **empirical field paper** (author's contribution).

Core idea: platform solicitation policy determines what a rating measures. Yelp forbids businesses from
asking for reviews; Google permits it; so the same establishment carries two ratings under opposing rules.
Tests use published star-rating histograms, Yelp's not-recommended pile, and LLM-scored review text, to
ask (i) whether Google businesses split into the two strategies and (ii) whether the split tracks quality.

Companion analysis: `scripts/python/scraping/` (platform data), `scripts/python/classifier/` (LLM text
scorer + validation), `scripts/stata/` (distributional statistics). Source: the proposal `.docx`
(to be added to `docs/`). Cite Gao et al. (2015, "Vocal Minority and Silent Majority") early and say how
the question differs.
