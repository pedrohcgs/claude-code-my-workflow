# Present a research paper

Read the paper end to end and produce a presentation plan calibrated to the named audience and time limit. If either is absent, use a research-seminar audience and 20 minutes.

Include:

1. A one-sentence research question and contribution.
2. The motivating puzzle and the closest literature.
3. Data, research design, estimand, and identifying assumptions.
4. Three to five results worth showing, with intuition, uncertainty, and exact source locations.
5. The strongest limitation or alternative interpretation.
6. A timed slide outline for Beamer or another user-selected research format.
7. Discussion questions aimed at methods, interpretation, and extensions.

Do not fabricate findings or citations. Distinguish what the paper demonstrates from what the authors claim. Preserve notation from the paper and flag unresolved ambiguities.
