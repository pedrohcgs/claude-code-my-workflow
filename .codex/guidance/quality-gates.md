# Quality gates

Research presentations must compile with no undefined citations/references, material overflow, unreadable type, missing figures, broken TikZ, unsupported major claims, or inconsistent notation.

Manuscripts and analysis must compile or pass tests; reported numbers must trace to generated outputs or documented sources; methodological claims must survive the relevant review; and changed claims must have no unresolved FAIL or STALE passport entries.

Critical findings and HIGH-WARN discrepancies block commit. Medium findings require explicit disposition. Low findings may remain only when reported. A score never overrides a hard failure. Re-run a failed gate after fixes.
