## Key Papers — Strand 1 (economic / computational / monetizable-value approaches)

### Coyle, D. & Manley, A. (2024) — "What Is the Value of Data? A Review of Empirical Methods"
- **Venue:** Journal of Economic Surveys, 2024.
- **Main contribution:** Reviews cost-based (production/maintenance cost), income-based, and
  market-based methods for valuing data; concludes no consensus standard method exists.
- **Relevance:** Directly grounds §3a's opening claim about cost-based/income-based/market-based
  valuation approaches. Already cited in §1 of the manuscript — same source, reused correctly.

### Mohan, S.K., Bharathy, G. & Jalan, A. (2026) — "Enterprise Data Valuation—A Targeted Literature Review"
- **Venue:** Journal of Economic Surveys, 40(1), pp. 73–92.
- **Main contribution:** Reviews enterprise/intangible-asset data valuation approaches — customer
  lifetime value, shareholder value, cooperative game theory (Shapley values), ML-based methods.
- **Relevance:** NEW addition. Current (2026), directly on-topic for Strand 1, and broadens the
  citation base beyond a single review paper.

## Key Papers — Strand 2 (contextual / relational / use-dependent value)

### Vargo, S.L. & Lusch, R.F. (2004) — "Evolving to a New Dominant Logic for Marketing"
- **Venue:** Journal of Marketing, 68(1), pp. 1–17.
- **Main contribution:** Foundational service-dominant-logic paper — value is co-created, not
  embedded in a good; marks the shift from goods-dominant to service-dominant thinking.

### Vargo, S.L. & Lusch, R.F. (2008) — "Service-Dominant Logic: Continuing the Evolution"
- **Venue:** Journal of the Academy of Marketing Science, 36(1), pp. 1–10. DOI 10.1007/s11747-007-0069-6.
- **Main contribution:** Updates the original foundational premises of S-D logic.

### Grönroos, C. (2011) — "Value Co-creation in Service Logic: A Critical Analysis"
- **Venue:** Marketing Theory, 11(3), pp. 279–301.
- **Main contribution:** Value-in-use is generated by the customer at the moment and in the
  context of use; firms facilitate rather than directly create value.
- **Note:** A companion piece, Grönroos & Voima (2011, working paper; later 2013 JAMS article),
  covers similar ground but is co-authored — the solo 2011 Marketing Theory paper is the better
  match for a single-author "Grönroos (2011)" citation.

### Hunke, F., Heinz, D. & Satzger, G. (2021) — "Creating Customer Value from Data: Foundations and Archetypes of Analytics-Based Services"
- **Venue:** Electronic Markets, 32, pp. 503–521. DOI 10.1007/s12525-021-00506-y.
- **Main contribution:** Defines analytics-based services; proposes four archetypal data-driven
  business models (data provider, insight provider, recommendation provider, digital solution
  provider) built around applying data to a task rather than possessing it.

### Xu, D., Indulska, M., Asadi Someh, I. & Shanks, G. (2024) — "Time to reassess data value: The many faces of data in organizations"
- **Venue:** Journal of Strategic Information Systems, Vol. 33 (October 2024).
- **Main contribution:** Conceptualizes multiple roles of data (tool, commodity, practice,
  algorithmic intelligence), each with a distinct value-creation pathway.
- **Note:** Full author list recovered — corrects the original draft's abbreviated "Xu, et al."

### Kvalvik, P., Misra, S., Mishra, B. & Dash, S. (2026) — "Data as an Asset: Economic Traits, Market Dynamics, and Monetization Challenges"
- **Venue:** WIREs Data Mining and Knowledge Discovery, 16(1). DOI 10.1002/widm.70072.
- **Main contribution:** Multivocal literature review (64 of 850 screened studies) of data's
  economic traits — non-rivalry, non-excludability, and scalability — and monetization
  challenges.
- **Correction:** the draft's "Kvalvik (2026)" omitted a fourth author (**Mishra**) and named
  "context-dependent worth" as a third trait where the paper actually names **scalability** —
  both confirmed by independent verification (Crossref, OpenAlex, Semantic Scholar agree) and
  corrected in the rewrite.

### Wixom, B.H., Beath, C.M. & Owens, L. (2023) — "Data Is Everybody's Business: The Fundamentals of Data Monetization"
- **Type:** Book (MIT Press), not a journal article — `.bib` entry type corrected to `@book`.
- **Main contribution:** Distinguishes creating value from data (fixing processes, improving
  efficiency) from realizing/monetizing that value (selling, improving, wrapping).

### Castro Fernandez, R. (2025) — "What is the Value of Data? A Theory and Systematization"
- **Venue:** ACM/IMS Journal of Data Science. DOI 10.1145/3728476.
- **Main contribution:** Distinguishes "data" (objective value, proportional to representational
  precision) from "documents" (subjective utility, task-dependent); frames the anchor of the
  manuscript's whole conceptual background.
