### Issue 1: Confirmed — run-on sentence / broken parallelism in §5 theoretical-contributions sentence (the flagged known issue)
- **Location:** §5 Expected Results, line 62
- **Current:** "the study operationalizes the microfoundations of value empirically, complementing institutional logics theory's blind spots ([@cloutierLangley2013]) turns Nabben's [-@nabben2025] relational concept of value into an empirically derived typology, and explains, through the instrumentation-asymmetry finding, not merely that justifications differ but why some come to dominate data-value disputes."
- **Proposed:** "the study operationalizes the microfoundations of value empirically, complements institutional logics theory's blind spots ([@cloutierLangley2013]), turns Nabben's [-@nabben2025] relational concept of value into an empirically derived typology, and explains, through the instrumentation-asymmetry finding, not merely that justifications differ but why some come to dominate data-value disputes."
